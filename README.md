### <code>PROJECT_01</code> — Green LLM Token Research

**Research | Sustainable AI · LLM Evaluation**

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Ollama](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ollama-000000?style=flat-square&logo=ollama&logoColor=white)
![Google Colab](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google_Colab-F9AB00?style=flat-square&logo=googlecolab&logoColor=white)
![JSONL / CSV](https://img.shields.io/badge/JSONL_/_CSV-555555?style=flat-square)

A research study on whether prompt optimization can cut unnecessary token use in student tasks without hurting answer quality.

**Experiment Setup**
- 100 tasks across 5 categories
- 4 models: Gemma 3 4B, Llama 3.2 3B, Qwen2.5 3B, Ministral 3 3B
- 800 primary runs at temperature 0, seed 42

**Early Results**
- **23.67%** fewer tokens on Gemma 3 4B
- **17.16%** fewer tokens on Ministral 3 3B

**Research Focus:** Token efficiency, prompt optimization, and answer quality in educational tasks.

[![View Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/VIEW_PROJECT-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/IbtidaAra5/sustainable_green-llm-token-research-edu)

### <code>PROJECT_02</code> — Bangla Traffic Law MCQ Dataset & Benchmark

**Research | Legal AI · Benchmark Dataset**

![Bangla](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bangla_Dataset-006A4E?style=flat-square)
![Legal AI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Legal_AI-555555?style=flat-square)
![Benchmark](https://img.shields.io/badge/Benchmark-4A90A4?style=flat-square)

A native Bangla multiple-choice benchmark built from the Bangladesh Road Transport Act, 2018, to test how well language models know a local statute. Built from the official government text with manual verification.

**Core Components**
- **299** hand-checked MCQs, each written by one team member and verified by another
- Direct-recall and scenario-style questions
- Simple three-part scoring: accuracy, factual error, hallucination

**Research Focus:** Local-statute knowledge, hallucination, and Bangla-language evaluation.

[![View Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/VIEW_PROJECT-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/IbtidaAra5/BanglaTrafficLaw-MCQ-Dataset-Benchmark)

**HCI Research | Consent-Aware AI Companion**

An HCI project (Group Orange) on how an AI companion should handle a shift from friendly to romantic interaction.

**Core Tasks**

- Respond to a romantic shift
- Slow or stop it

**Study Results**
